An efficient bug-finding technique for asynchronous concurrent programs is **systematic concurrency testing**, especially **randomized scheduling / stress testing**.

Why it helps:
- Bugs in async/concurrent code often depend on rare interleavings.
- By repeatedly varying task scheduling, delays, and execution order, you can expose race conditions, deadlocks, and ordering bugs much faster than with ordinary testing.

- **Randomized scheduler testing**: deliberately perturb task order.
- **Systematic exploration of interleavings**: explore many possible execution orders in a controlled way.
- **Bounded model checking / stateless model checking**: search for problematic interleavings within a bounded depth.
- **Deterministic replay with perturbation**: reproduce and then slightly vary the schedule to find related bugs.
